● No, SIMD/AVX2 vectorization is NOT implemented in the current codebase.

  What we DO have:
  - Portable popcount/ctz intrinsics (__popcnt/_BitScanForward on MSVC,
    __builtin_popcount/__builtin_ctz on Clang/GCC)
  - LTO enabled via CMake (/GL + /LTCG on MSVC, -flto/-flto=thin on Clang/GCC)
  - -march=native / /arch:AVX2 compiler flags (allows auto-vectorization)

  What's missing: Explicit SIMD intrinsics (__m256i, _mm256_andnot_si256, etc.)
  for processing 4-8 cells in parallel as shown in your snippet.

  The current solver works on one cell at a time in _findBestCell() and
  _solveRecursive(). Adding explicit AVX2 would require:
  - Restructuring candidate computation to operate on 4-8 cells simultaneously
  - Using __m256i for row/col/box mask arrays (currently Mask _rows[9], etc.)
  - Handling the 81-cell flat grid with 256-bit loads/stores

  Would you like me to add explicit AVX2 vectorization? It's a non-trivial
  refactor but could yield 2-4× speedup on the constraint propagation and
  candidate updates.
